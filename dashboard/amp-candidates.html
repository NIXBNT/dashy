How the averages are calculated

AMP mean gives equal weight to AMP Scanner v2, AMPidentifier, CAMPR3, and CAMPR4. Each CAMP server contributes the mean of its reported probabilities.

All numeric mean gives equal weight to those four server values and ClassAMP’s confidence in its predicted activity class. Use the AMP mean for general AMP ranking.

DBAASP and CAMPR3 ANN provide class labels only. Displayed scores are rounded to four decimals. Sorting uses full precision.
