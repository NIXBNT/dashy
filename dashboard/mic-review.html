MIC evidence review

Each numeric entry in the historical MIC matrix matches an original MIC table cell. The original extraction also recorded MBC from supplement Table S8. The audited analysis excludes that endpoint and preserves explicit greater-than limits, blank cells and untested assays separately.

Family-held-out MIC benchmark

The earlier suggestion to default to activity classification is premature. At the original cutoffs, the strongest family-held-out balanced accuracy for each strain ranged from 0.532 to 0.620. A shuffled-label benchmark frequently produced an equally strong winner. The PAO1 descriptor classifier, for example, identified only 1 of 11 active records while correctly classifying 35 of 36 inactive records.

Cohort. The source ledger yields 271 unique peptide–strain observations with exact values or explicit bounds. Seven lack matching descriptor rows, including four MLWH57 observations for which Table S7 gives a different sequence from the historical spreadsheet. Two exact MICs belong to a seventh strain with insufficient observations. The six-strain benchmark therefore uses 262 observations. Only 107 have both recorded iAMP and RF scores. Coverage is especially poor in the motif study. Missing scores receive each training fold's median. Missingness and source composition limit interpretation of score comparisons. Three >160 µM records lack matching descriptors. One >200 µM observation enters classification as inactive at every evaluated cutoff. Continuous fits and errors use exact observations only. Blank cells and qualitative inactivity without a numerical bound are excluded, so performance applies to this recorded subset.

Validation. Sequences linked by at least 80% normalized Levenshtein similarity form connected components. These groups approximate related series. Five deterministic group folds keep each component together. Five shuffled row folds provide a comparison on the same records. Each training fold fits its own descriptor projection (up to ten dimensions) and balanced logistic classifier. The R²-weighted consensus was fixed in advance. Across strains, descriptor balanced accuracy averages 0.565 under random splits and 0.562 under grouped splits. RF drops from 0.510 to 0.456. Grouping changes some comparisons but does not explain all of the weak performance.

Intervals resample groups 400 times from fixed held-out predictions. They omit training-set and model-selection uncertainty. The descriptive permutation check shuffles labels within study 99 times, refits all five classifiers on the same folds and compares the observed winner against the maximum for each shuffle. It preserves study prevalence but breaks within-family label dependence. These are approximate comparisons; external validation remains open.

Continuous MIC. On exact measurements, the six-strain mean absolute error of the training-median baseline is 1.60 twofold dilution steps, weighting strains equally. The descriptor ridge model averages 1.73 steps. It improves on the baseline for AB5075, ATCC43816 and PAO1 but loses enough on other strains to offset those gains. The unweighted and weighted score regressions both average 1.59. Continuous error gives a direct measure of the scale of disagreement even when a binary threshold is unstable.

Cutoffs, score disagreement and panel coverage

  • Halving the strain-specific cutoff changes 51 of 262 labels. Doubling it changes 43. For E. faecalis alone, lowering the cutoff moves 10 of 30 observations. Each threshold was evaluated on the same folds, with its own classifier fits.
  • The existing weighted consensus gives mean balanced accuracy 0.431 across strains versus 0.467 for the unweighted average. On the 107 observations with measured computational scores available, iAMP and RF give the same held-out classification on 56. Both are wrong on 34 of those 56. On the 51 disagreements, the weighted consensus makes 33 errors versus 30 for the unweighted average. Agreement offers little reassurance on this subset.
  • Revisiting the historical score overlay, ten holdout sequences have at least one exact MIC on the six-strain panel. The Spearman correlation of the recorded iAMP score with negative log2 of the lowest recorded MIC is 0.101, compared with −0.025 for RF. Restricting the overlay to S. aureus ATCC25923 leaves three sequences. Requiring all six strains leaves zero. The panel-matched association cannot be estimated from this holdout.
  • The next informative data work is to reconcile the MLWH57 descriptor identity, recover missing computational scores and establish what the blank source cells represent. A new independent, consistently measured panel would directly address the remaining uncertainty about generalization.
